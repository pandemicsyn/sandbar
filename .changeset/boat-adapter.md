Add the experimental `@sandbar-sdk/boat` adapter for Boat full-VM sandboxes: create with an idempotency key, argv/shell exec with exact output bytes and finite stdin, binary files up to 5 MiB, archive/resume, renewal and confirmed permanent delete. The CLI recognizes `--provider boat` with `BOAT_API_KEY` and `BOAT_NETWORK=internet`.
